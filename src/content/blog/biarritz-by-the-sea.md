Today we had a walking into the seaside town of Biarritz in the south west of France. It's been nice to break up the driving with a two day stop in a lovely part of France. 

Sunny and calm the town give me a surfer vibe like Newquay back In the UK but hot!! It's a pricey town don't get me wrong but a lovely coastline with lovely walks along the beach and cafes to get your daily espresso.

We stayed at Bela Basque a nice resort campsite that has static homes and spots for tents and caravans to stop over with a lovely pool and cafe. A really good eat stop.
